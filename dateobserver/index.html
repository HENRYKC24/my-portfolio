Supporting DateObserver

How supporting works

DateObserver is free and ad-free, built and maintained by one developer. Supporting it is welcome, and entirely up to you.

  • Supporting is voluntary. Everything in DateObserver works without paying, and it stays free.
  • Supporter perks, such as taking the DateObserver branding off the days you post, are a thank-you, not something you buy. They may change or end at any time.
  • Subscriptions: the perks last while you're subscribed, on any phone signed in to the same Google account. Manage or cancel anytime in Google Play.
  • One-time gifts: the perks last 12 months, on that phone only. They are kept only on that phone, so uninstalling the app, clearing its data or changing phones ends them, and they can't be restored.
  • Bank transfers can't be linked to the app, so they don't come with perks.
  • Payments, renewals and refunds are handled by Google Play, under its own policies.
