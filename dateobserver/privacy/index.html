DateObserver Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

DateObserver ("the app") is a calendar of observances, holidays and Igbo market days, with reminders, a to-do list and a clock. It is made by Henry Kc ("I", "me"), an independent developer in Nigeria. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app. It replaces the policy dated 27 August 2023.

In short: DateObserver does not collect, store or sell your personal information. What you put into the app stays on your phone.

1. Information the app keeps on your phone

The app keeps the following only on your device:

  • your reminders, alarms, to-dos and their times;
  • your settings and choices (your country, the countries you follow, your interests, theme, notification times, how lists are ordered, your branding choices for posts);
  • whether you are a supporter and, for a one-time gift, until when its thank-you perks last;
  • simple counters the app uses for itself (for example, how many days in a row you have opened it, so it knows when to show a message).

This information is not sent to me or to anyone else. It is not backed up: uninstalling the app, clearing its data or moving to a new phone removes it, and I cannot restore it.

2. Information the app does not collect

DateObserver has no accounts and no sign-in. It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or files. It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

3. When the app uses the internet

The app works offline. It goes online only when you ask it to:

  • Read more opens a web search (Google) about an observance or holiday in a page inside the app, or in your browser. What happens on those websites is governed by their own privacy policies.
  • Post it and Share pass a picture and words that you can see and edit to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp, Facebook, X, Instagram, TikTok or Messenger). The app does not post anything itself, and it receives nothing back. What you post is governed by that app's terms and privacy policy.
  • Supporting DateObserver through Google Play: payments are made through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment and your payment details; I do not see or store them. Google tells the app only whether a purchase or subscription is active. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • App updates and ratings use Google Play's own in-app update and review features.

4. Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications, alarms and reminders, run at start-up, full-screen alarms, vibration and keeping the phone awake: so your reminders, alarms, timer and daily note arrive on time, even after the phone restarts.
  • Internet and network status: for the features in section 3, and to tell you when you are offline.
  • Advertising ID: declared by the app, but DateObserver shows no ads and does not read or use it.

You can turn notifications and other permissions off at any time in your phone's settings.

5. Children

DateObserver is a general calendar, not directed at children under 13. It does not collect personal information from anyone, children included. If you believe a child has sent me personal information (for example by email), contact me and I will delete it.

6. Security

Because your information stays on your phone, it is protected by your phone's own security (its screen lock and encryption). Payments are protected by Google Play. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, but the app keeps nothing on servers that could be exposed.

7. Links to other websites and apps

Read more, Post it, Share and the Support page can open other websites and apps (such as Google Search, WhatsApp, Facebook or Google Play). I don't control them and am not responsible for their content or their privacy practices; please read their own policies.

8. Your rights

Because the app does not collect personal information, there is nothing held about you that I could show, correct or delete. If you email me (for example through Feedback in the app), I use your message and email address only to reply to you, and I delete them on request. Everything the app keeps is on your phone, and you can remove it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. You can contact me with any question about your privacy, including under Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app.
