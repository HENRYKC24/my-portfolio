How

Adding a calendar

  1. Google Calendar: tap Add to Google Calendar, then Add. On an Android phone this opens Google Calendar in your browser. Afterwards the calendar shows in the Google Calendar app too. If it doesn't, open the app's Settings, tap the calendar's name and turn on Sync.
  2. iPhone, iPad or Mac: tap Add to iPhone or Mac, then Subscribe.
  3. Outlook: tap Add to Outlook and sign in to Outlook.com.
  4. Any other calendar: copy the calendar link and paste it where your calendar says “subscribe” or “add from URL”.

To remove one, delete it in your calendar's settings, like any other calendar.
