About

I'm a software engineer from Nigeria. I design, build and ship products end to end: from the first sketch and the data model to the native code, the tests and the store listing. I care most about software that is useful, fast and kind to the people who use it.

My latest product is DateObserver, a calendar of the world's holidays and observances on Google Play. It works out the dates of festivals from the movements of the Sun and Moon, holds more than 11,000 observances a year from 199 countries, and runs entirely on the phone.

I studied full-stack software engineering at Microverse, graduating with 99.6% in the final assessment, and I also train aspiring developers online and in person. Teaching keeps my fundamentals sharp and my explanations clear.
