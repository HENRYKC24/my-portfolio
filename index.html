About

I'm a full-stack software engineer from Enugu, Nigeria, with 15+ years in tech: from teaching computer applications and running an IT unit, to building mobile and web products for remote teams in the United States, to co-founding a startup as its CTO.

DateObserver began in a classroom. While teaching the JavaScript Date object, I noticed how neatly the four-day Igbo market week sits beside the seven-day week, and set out to bring both together in one calendar. It has grown into a calendar of the whole world: more than 11,000 observances a year from 199 countries, with festivals worked out from the movements of the Sun and Moon.

I value respect, integrity, teamwork and growth, and I'm easy-going to work with. Away from the keyboard you'll find me enjoying football, a game that taught me discipline and determination. I also teach on my YouTube channel, Coding With KC.

My dream is to keep building things that help people, and one day to give back as a philanthropist.
